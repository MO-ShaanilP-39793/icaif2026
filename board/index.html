How it ranks

In every rolling 15-day window of the holdout, each entrant is ranked on cumulative return and Sharpe (higher is better) and on maximum drawdown and turnover (lower is better), with ties sharing the average rank. The Overall Rank Score is the mean of the four ranks, and the board orders entrants by its mean across windows. The references are three fixed strategies: cash, equal-weight buy-and-hold and inverse-volatility at 75% gross. Only each strategy's newest version is ranked; every version is listed below the board.
