## Why the code changed (one commit on main)

The live runner's last check (`runner.guard`) refused any trade whose total turnover is under
`MIN_TURNOVER = 0.005` as "drift, not a decision". A 0.1%-invested entry turns over 0.001,
so the guard would have held, uploaded nothing, and logged only `hold`. The fix exempts a
book that is actually all cash (read from the book, not from a flag): a cash book has no
drift, so any buy from it is the decision. Once the book holds shares the floor applies as
before. Nothing else in the path blocks a small book: the weight grid and the "all cash"
threshold are 1e-6, and at 0.1% the smallest names sit near 1e-4 of NAV ($100-300 each).

Not tested live: no phase has uploaded a book this small. Fractional shares are allowed and
the rules state no minimum order, but if the server's receipt shows a failed or partial
execution, the alert watcher fires and the fallback is to rerun the next round at 0.01.
